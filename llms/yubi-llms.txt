# Yubi

> Yubi (formerly CredAvenue) is an Indian debt and credit technology company operating a full-stack digital marketplace that connects enterprises, lenders, and investors across India's credit ecosystem. It exposes self-serve APIs for co-lending and loan-origination data exchange through its developer portal.

Yubi's products include Yubi Loans (corporate borrowing), Yubi Co.Lend (bank/NBFC co-lending with one-time API integration), Yubi Pools (securitisation / ABS deal-making), Yubi Invest (fixed-income and debt investing), and Yubi Flow (supply-chain financing). Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Chennai; reached unicorn status in 2022. Backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners.
